# PRD 05: The relocation practicalities layer

Status: draft, awaiting go · 2026-11-05
Depends on: nothing. Feeds: demand for 01 and 04.

## Purpose
The boring, heavily searched layer of leaving a city: visas, healthcare, banking, internet reliability, and real monthly costs, per town, in English and Spanish. A pure AEO play. Every reader is a Slow Stays lead by definition.

## Why now
Everyone who searches "temporary resident visa Mérida" is about to move, and nobody owns those answers with structured, maintained, bilingual content. The search volume exists today and grows with the trend.

## Who it is for
People in the research phase, 3 to 12 months before they move.

## v1 scope
- 10 town guides times 5 evergreen topics: visa path, healthcare, banking, internet, cost of a month from Market Watch data.
- Published on the quiet-places site as the canonical home of the whole set: guides, llms.txt, sitemap, and hreflang pairs build as one unit, with structured data, EN and ES. Ancuria links to the guides for authority instead of hosting them. (Decision 2026-11-19, supersedes the earlier "published on Ancuria" wording; the directory's own domain still lands per PRD 03.)
- Each guide ends with one clear next step: the Slow Stays page or a WhatsApp line.
- Quarterly refresh per town, run by an automation pass with human review.

## Out of scope for v1
Affiliate deals with insurers or banks until traffic exists. Legal advice: we describe processes, we do not represent. User-generated content.

## Flow
A search or an assistant question lands on a guide, the guide answers fully, the reader self-selects into Slow Stays or a Test-Drive.

## Built from
The SEO/AEO machinery, Market Watch data, the quarterly-refresh automation pattern already running on the fleet.

## Money
Indirect by design: the cheapest leads 01 and 04 will ever get. Affiliates come later.

## Metrics
5 guides live and indexed within 45 days, 10 within 90, first AI citation, 5% of guide readers clicking through to Slow Stays.

## Risks
Content drift, mitigated by the refresh pass. Thin data in new towns: be honest and show what we know.

## First milestone
5 guides live, indexed, and wired into the Slow Stays funnel.
