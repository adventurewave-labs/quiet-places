# PRD 03: The quiet-places directory

Status: draft, awaiting go · 2026-11-05
Depends on: nothing to seed, grows from 01. Feeds: 09.

## Purpose
Become the canonical answer to "where do I go to log off": a structured, bilingual directory of artist residencies, wellness hubs, retreat houses, and calm towns, starting in Mexico. Built AEO-first so the AI assistants of 2030 cite it. No incumbent owns this category yet.

## Why now
When people retreat from public feeds they still need to find each other, and they will ask an assistant, not scroll a hashtag. Whoever owns the structured answer owns the category. The directory also gives every other PRD a discovery surface.

## Who it is for
The same people as PRD 01, plus hub owners who want to be found without running a public feed.

## v1 scope
- Seed as a section of the Slow Stays page: 25 entries, each with town, focus (art, wellness, craft, retreat), language, price band, contact channel.
- Structured data, llms.txt, EN and ES pages, static and fast, per the estate's html-sites pattern.
- Free inclusion for hubs, one canonical page each, no feed, no reviews.

## Out of scope for v1
Its own domain, spun out when the section passes about 50 entries. Ratings, reviews, booking; contact hands off to the hub's own channel or to PRD 01.

## Flow
An assistant or a human asks where to go, the directory's structured pages answer, contact happens on the hub's own channel, usually WhatsApp, which matches the trend instead of fighting it.

## Built from
The gist data layer and launchpad pattern from the html-sites lane, the 20-site SEO/AEO machinery, Market Watch sweep passes that already visit these towns and can capture candidates.

## Money
Long-game moat: strategic data now, featured placement later. It feeds 01, 04, and 09 with demand.

## Metrics
25 entries at launch, 50 within 6 months, one verifiable AI citation for a quiet-towns query, first organic WhatsApp contact attributed to it.

## Risks
Content upkeep, mitigated by only adding hubs verifiable in a sweep. The category may be early; seeding is cheap, so acceptable.

## First milestone
25-entry seed section live with structured data and llms.txt.
