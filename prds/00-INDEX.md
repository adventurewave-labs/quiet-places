# Log-off economy PRDs, 2026-11-05

Fourteen PRDs from the quiet-hubs trend brainstorm: people retreating from big cities and public feeds into small, local, hyper-specific artistic and therapeutic hubs through the 2030s. All propose-only. Nothing is built. Pick a number and say go.

Conventions across the whole set: bilingual EN/ES by default, WhatsApp-first contact, AEO-first pages, estate reuse before new builds, no em dashes in anything customer-facing.
