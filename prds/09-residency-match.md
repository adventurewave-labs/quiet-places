# PRD 09: Residency match

Status: draft, awaiting go · 2026-11-05
Depends on: 03 for supply visibility, 02 for hub ops. Feeds: 12.

## Purpose
Two-sided placement: hubs need artists and practitioners in residence, practitioners need places to land. Hand-matched at first, productized later. Nobody serves this well in Latin America.

## Why now
Every hub the trend creates needs programming and pulse; every practitioner needs affordable space and an audience. The match is high-trust and local, which is exactly what a feed cannot do and a curator can.

## Who it is for
Practitioners: artists, teachers, therapists, writers. Hubs: the directory's supply side.

## v1 scope
- Two intake forms, practitioner and hub, bilingual, on the directory site.
- Manual matching by us: review, WhatsApp both sides, agree terms, weeks, space, stipend or fee, and what the hub gets.
- A flat placement fee per match, paid by the hub or split, set per match.
- A public page of past residencies as social proof for both sides.

## Out of scope for v1
Automated matching, contracts and payments tooling, visa sponsorship, a self-serve marketplace.

## Flow
A practitioner applies, a hub posts a need, we match within two weeks, the residency happens, both sides report back, the page records it.

## Built from
Directory entries (PRD 03) as the hub roster, hub-in-a-box members (PRD 02) as the audience for open calls, the WhatsApp flow for all comms.

## Money
$100 to $250 placement fee per match per side at v1 volume. The point is proving the match rate, not the revenue.

## Metrics
10 practitioner profiles and 5 hub needs on file in 90 days, 3 placements in 6 months, both sides willing to do it again.

## Risks
Two-sided cold start, mitigated by recruiting both sides by hand from people we already know. Visa limits on paid work: keep v1 residencies stipend-based.

## First milestone
Three matches made manually, documented, and referenceable.
