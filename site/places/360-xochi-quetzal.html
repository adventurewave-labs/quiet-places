360 Xochi Quetzal

A private residency for artists, writers, and musicians on Lake Chapala, an hour from Guadalajara. Sessions are self directed: residents get a room, workspace, and a small creative community, and bring their own project. The town has a large expat and artist population, weekly markets, and a mild climate through the year.
