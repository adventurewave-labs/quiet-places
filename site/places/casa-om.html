Casa Om

A retreat house near Puerto Morelos, a fishing village between Cancun and Playa del Carmen. Casa Om centers on a yoga shala and a heated pool, hosts teacher led group weeks and training programs, and keeps the pace slow with shared dinners. The reef offshore is a national park a short swim out.
