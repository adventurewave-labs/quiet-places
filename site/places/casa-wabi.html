Casa Wabi

A foundation on the Oaxacan coast near Puerto Escondido, founded by artist Bosco Sodi. Casa Wabi hosts residencies for artists of many disciplines on a site that includes pavilions by noted architects, a clay kiln, and an open program for local schools. Stays are by application and the calendar follows seasonal sessions.
