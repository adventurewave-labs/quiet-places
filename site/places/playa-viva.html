Playa Viva

A regenerative resort on a private stretch of coast near Juluchuca, between Zihuatanejo and Manzanillo. Playa Viva runs on solar power, works with the local community on reef and turtle projects, and hosts both guests and group retreats in casitas built from local materials. The estuary behind the beach draws birds year round.
