Xinalani Retreat

A beachfront yoga retreat south of Puerto Vallarta, reached by boat from the city. Xinalani has palm roofed rooms, several yoga decks facing the ocean, a spa, and healthy dining, and it hosts both its own retreats and visiting teacher groups. No road reaches the beach, which keeps the cove quiet.
