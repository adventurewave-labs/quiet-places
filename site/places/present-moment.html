Present Moment Retreat

A beachfront retreat in Troncones, a quiet surf village on the Guerrero coast north of Ixtapa. Present Moment pairs daily yoga and meditation with surfing, spa treatments, and communal meals, and it runs both personal stays and hosted group weeks. The village has no high rises and the beach is the main road.
