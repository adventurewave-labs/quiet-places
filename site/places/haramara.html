Haramara Retreat

A yoga retreat center on a hillside above Sayulita beach in Nayarit. Haramara runs its own calendar of retreats and hosts visiting teachers, with open air yoga palapas, hand built casitas, a saltwater pool, and a dining room keyed to the retreat schedule. The grounds are pedestrian only and town is a short walk.
