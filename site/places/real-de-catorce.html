Real de Catorce

A former silver town high in the mountains of San Luis Potosí, reached through a long tunnel. Real de Catorce keeps cobblestone streets, ruins, and a Huichol pilgrimage route to Wirikuta nearby. Cold nights, empty mornings, and the desert beyond make it a place people go to be offline on purpose.
