Sanoviv Medical Institute

A residential health institute on the coast south of Rosarito in Baja California. Sanoviv runs week long programs that combine medical assessment, nutrition, movement, and spa therapy in one campus, with lodging and meals included. It suits people who want a structured reset under medical supervision rather than a free form retreat.
