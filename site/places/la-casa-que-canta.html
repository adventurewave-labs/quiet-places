La Casa que Canta

A boutique hotel built into the cliffs above Playa La Ropa, on Zihuatanejo bay in Guerrero. La Casa que Canta has sea facing suites, a saltwater pool at the edge of the rock, terraced decks over the water, and a quiet adults oriented atmosphere. It suits slow coastal stays and small celebrations.
