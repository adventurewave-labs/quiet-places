Tepoztlán

A market town below the Tepozteco cliffs about an hour south of Mexico City. Tepoztlán has drawn seekers, artists, and healers for a century, with weekend markets, temazcales, yoga spaces, and hikes to the pyramid on the cliff. It is a Pueblo Mágico and a classic first stop out of the capital.
