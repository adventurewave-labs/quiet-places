Playa Escondida

A hideaway of palapa roofed bungalows in the jungle between Sayulita and San Francisco, Nayarit. Playa Escondida is adults only, with a private beach path, a pool built into the rocks, yoga on its own deck, and restaurant meals by lantern light. It hosts small retreat groups and individual stays.
