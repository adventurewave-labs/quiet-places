Bacalar

A small town on the lagoon of seven colors in southern Quintana Roo, far from the Riviera Maya crowds. Bacalar life moves between the water, the fort, and the town square, with stilted docks, morning swims, and dark skies at night. A magnet for slow travelers and kitesurfers in season.
