Verana

A boutique hotel above the boat access village of Yelapa, south of Puerto Vallarta. Verana sits in the jungle with views down to the bay, a small spa, and a pool, and it hosts individual stays and full house bookings for groups. There are no cars in Yelapa, which keeps nights quiet.
