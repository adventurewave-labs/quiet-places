Teotitlán del Valle

A Zapotec weaving village in the Tlacolula valley east of Oaxaca city. Teotitlán del Valle is known for wool rugs dyed with natural pigments, family workshops that welcome visitors, and a slow calendar of saints days and markets. Several families take guests for weaving courses by arrangement.
