Mata Ortiz

A village on the edge of the Sierra Madre in northern Chihuahua, known worldwide for its hand built pottery. Mata Ortiz ceramics grew from the work of Juan Quezada and now supports dozens of family studios. The pace is slow, the desert is close, and artists sell from their homes.
