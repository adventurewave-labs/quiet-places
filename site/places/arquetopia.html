Arquetopia

An international artist residency foundation with programs in Puebla and Oaxaca. Arquetopia runs themed sessions in ceramic, textile, printmaking, and writing, hosted in colonial buildings with dedicated studio space and staff. Programs run from two weeks upward and draw artists, scholars, and curators from many countries. Instruction is in Spanish and English.
