Guapamacátaro Art and Ecology

An art and ecology residency held on a former hacienda in rural Michoacán, between Morelia and Quiroga. Sessions gather a small cohort around themes like land, food systems, and community, with studios, a library, and fields to walk. The program is bilingual and sized for deep work rather than output.
