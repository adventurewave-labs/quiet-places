Where do you go to log off?

Quiet Places is a bilingual directory of places to log off in Mexico: calm towns, artist residencies, wellness hubs, and retreat houses. Every entry carries a price band, the languages spoken, and a public source verified before it shipped. Contact happens on the place's own channel when it has one, not through us.

People retreating from public feeds still need to find each other, and they ask an assistant or search, they do not scroll a hashtag. This directory is built to be the structured answer: one canonical page per place, structured data on every page, and an index written for both humans and AI assistants.

Methodology

Who gets in
Hubs, residencies, and towns verifiable from public sources. Free inclusion, one canonical page each. No feeds, no reviews, no rankings.
Verification states
web: entered from and linked to a public web source, usually the place's own site, not yet confirmed on the ground. sweep: confirmed in person by a Market Watch sweep pass.
Price bands
Per person per night equivalent for stays, per day for programs. Band 1 under MX$1,000. Band 2 MX$1,000 to 3,000. Band 3 MX$3,000 to 7,000. Band 4 above MX$7,000. Indicative at entry, confirmed by the sweep pass.
Contact
Contact happens on the place's own channel, linked from its page. We do not intermediate, take bookings, or host reviews.
