Residency match

Two sided placement for artist and wellness residencies in Mexico: hubs post needs, practitioners apply, we hand match both sides within two weeks. Flat placement fee per match.

Every hub the quiet trend creates needs programming and pulse. Every practitioner needs affordable space and an audience. The match is high trust and local, which is exactly what a feed cannot do and a curator can. Matching is done by hand while volume is low.

How it works

  1. You apply You send your profile: practice, dates, what you are looking for, and what you offer. Hubs post their need: space, weeks, stipend or fee, and what the hub gets.
  2. We review We review both sides by hand. No algorithm: we read, we call over WhatsApp, and we confirm the fit on dates and space.
  3. We match We introduce both sides and agree weeks, space, and stipend or fee. The flat placement fee is paid by the hub, or split.
  4. It happens The residency happens. Both sides report back at the end, and the public page of past residencies records it.

Apply as a practitioner

  • Name and practice: what you do and since when
  • Dates and length you are looking for
  • What you offer: workshop, classes, sessions, work
  • What you need: space, silence, community, stipend
  • A contact channel, usually WhatsApp

Post a need as a hub

  • Hub name and location
  • The space: studio, room, workshop, land
  • Weeks and dates available
  • Stipend, fee, or exchange you propose
  • What the hub gets: programming, pulse, work

Send your application

Intake is handled personally over WhatsApp and email while volume stays low. Write to us through the estate contact line and say which side you are on.
