Prana del Mar

A yoga and wellness retreat center on the Pacific side of the Cabo corridor, near the village of El Pescadero. Prana del Mar has a large oceanfront shala, suites and garden rooms, an organic garden, and a pool, and it hosts visiting teacher groups and personal retreats in season.
