Holistika Tulum

A wellness community and hotel set back from the beach road in the Tulum jungle. Holistika has a yoga shala, temazcal, vegetarian kitchen, and art installations across the grounds, and it runs classes, therapies, and retreats that guests and day visitors can join. It keeps its own quiet inside a busy town.
