San Bartolo Coyotepec

The home of barro negro, the black pottery of Oaxaca, just south of the city. San Bartolo Coyotepec works clay in family talleres where the burnishing technique passed down through generations is still done by hand. Visitors can watch the kilns, try the wheel, and buy direct from the makers.
