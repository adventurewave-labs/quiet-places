Mar de Jade

A family run wellness retreat on Chacala beach in Nayarit, operating since the 1980s. Mar de Jade combines daily yoga and meditation with spa services, surf, and volunteer work with local clinics and farms. It hosts visiting groups and individual guests, and the rooms open onto the sand or the garden.
